Privacy Policy

Effective 20 July 2026

This policy explains how the emcpp mobile app (“the app”) handles information. emcpp is a suite of electromagnetic-compatibility and RF engineering calculators, published by Semih Ufuk Güler. In short: the app collects no personal data and does not track you.

01What the app does

You enter numeric engineering parameters (for example a frequency, a length, or a material property). The app sends those numbers to the emcpp compute service over a secure HTTPS connection, which calculates a result and returns it for display. That is the app’s entire network activity.

02Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts and asks for no personal information. It does not collect or access your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for tracking, or any advertising identifier. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

03The parameters you send

The only data transmitted are the numeric values you type in order to compute a result. They are sent solely to return that result and are not linked to your identity or used to build a profile. The compute service may keep standard, short-lived technical logs (such as a timestamp and the request status) for reliability and security; these logs are not used to identify you and are not sold or shared.

04Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. The numeric parameters are processed only by the emcpp compute service, which is our own infrastructure, for the sole purpose of returning your calculation.

05Data retention

Because the app collects no personal data, there is no personal profile to retain. Calculation inputs are processed to return your result and are not stored as personal data. Operational server logs, where present, are retained only briefly and then discarded on a rolling basis.

06Children

The app is a professional engineering tool, is not directed at children, and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

07Your choices

Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to request access to, correct, or delete. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at the address below.

08Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the text and the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

09Contact
